Code for different auton versions; paste it into auton_sequence() and download to brain
Make sure you fully delete the previous sequence from the function before that
Ramming into a stack of rings makes you eat the lower one

DEFAULT - place as far from wall as possible. just move straight to let the other team do stuff
auton_move_straight(10)

TOUCH LADDER - place as far from wall as posible, robot side touching tile edge closer to mid
auton_move_straight(90)
